1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the 幻兔 (SnapFlow) app and its account, content, album, recommendation and messaging services. It is published by the app developer. Send privacy, access, correction or deletion requests to dreamylost@outlook.com. We process information only for the purposes described here.

SnapFlow uses cloud services; not all data stays on your device. Profile details, works and interactions you make public are visible to their intended audiences. Do not include passwords, identity documents, other people’s private information or unnecessary sensitive information in public content.

2. Accounts and authentication

Phone registration or login sends your country code, phone number and entered password to our service to create an account, authenticate you and maintain a session. Changing your login password requires your old and new passwords; the server verifies the old password and stores a salted hash of the new one. The app does not save your entered passwords. Do not reuse passwords from other important services. With Sign in with Apple or Apple account linking, we process the identifier, verification credentials and the email address you authorize Apple to provide and our server verifies, to link your sign-in identity and display it in Account and Security. If you choose Hide My Email, Apple may provide a relay address. We do not use email to automatically merge accounts or display it in public profiles, and we do not receive your Apple Account password.

We process your account ID, chosen nickname, avatar, biography and necessary login, security and permission records. Network services receive information such as IP addresses and request times for operation, abuse prevention and IP-region features where offered. An IP-derived region is not your device’s precise location.

3. Photos, camera and local organization

You control system photo and camera permissions for selecting, taking, previewing, saving and organizing photos. You can use limited photo access where offered and change permissions in system settings. Refusal affects the relevant feature rather than unrelated features. Local classification, similar-photo analysis and cleanup read authorized media and metadata; their results and caches stay on the device. System photo deletion remains subject to system authorization and confirmation.

When you upload photos, add them to a cloud album or enable synchronization of a selected album, selected files and related metadata are sent to our servers and storage provider. Metadata can include capture time, camera/lens details, EXIF and coordinates already present in a photo. This does not continuously collect your live location. Location embedded in an original photo can also travel with a shared file; remove information you do not want to disclose before uploading or sharing.

Granting library access does not itself publicly upload your whole library. Cloud-album access and sharing depend on your actions and permissions. Album synchronization you choose continues processing new content within that task’s scope until you turn it off or cancel it.

4. Works, interactions and content safety

We store submitted works, images, videos, text, comments, likes, favorites, follows and necessary associations to publish and display content, share albums, review submissions, deliver interaction notices, retain reading/playback/game progress and handle feedback. Reports, feedback and attachments you submit are used to investigate issues and maintain service safety.

Content is shown according to its visibility, album membership or authorized share-link access. Anonymous works hide the author’s public identity in the relevant interface; they do not prevent the developer from associating a work with its account for permission checks, moderation and abuse prevention. Authorized personnel may access necessary content for review, reports or support requests.

The app does not currently provide a feature that automatically submits private albums to third-party generative AI. Before any future sharing of personal information with a third-party AI service, we will identify the recipient, information and purpose and obtain explicit authorization as required before transfer. This policy is not blanket consent to such future processing.

5. Recommendations, usage records and local identifiers

Recommendation and resume features process browsing, searches, interactions, viewing/listening progress or duration, recommendation requests and negative feedback generated through use of the service. These records support content delivery, duplicate avoidance, progress restoration and troubleshooting and may be associated with your account. Existing history, recommendation-preference and content controls let you manage the relevant records or preferences.

The current production build enables first-party feature analytics, processing event times, a random installation identifier, session/page identifiers, content or card identifiers and feature actions for recommendations, operation and troubleshooting. Weather coordinates, city names and IP addresses are not sent as weather feature event fields. The current app includes no third-party advertising SDK or cross-app advertising tracking SDK, does not read IDFA for advertising tracking and does not sell personal information.

6. Messages and iOS notifications

Direct messaging processes the text and emoji you send, sender and recipient identifiers, conversations, timestamps, read receipts, recall and personal deletion state to deliver messages and synchronize your devices. Chat history may also be stored on your device. Direct messages and notifications are not end-to-end encrypted. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

We process follow relationships, message acceptance scope, blocking, mute, pin and conversation visibility preferences to control messaging permissions and alerts. When online status is enabled, foreground devices periodically renew a short-lived presence lease. Presence is shared only when both users follow each other, both enable visibility and neither has blocked the other. You can disable online status. Deleting or clearing a chat affects only your own records, not the recipient’s copies; recall cannot guarantee removal of content already viewed, copied or captured in screenshots.

Our messaging service processes interaction and review-result notices and read/deletion state, which may also be cached locally. With iOS notifications enabled, we and the messaging service process Apple’s device token, account binding, app/platform and push-environment information to deliver to the correct device and update or unregister a registration. A device token is not an Apple Account password, may change and is not used for cross-app ad tracking.

Notifications are delivered through Apple Push Notification service (APNs). They may contain nicknames, work titles or interaction/review summaries. System and app settings jointly control lock-screen display and previews; disable them as needed. Turning off system notifications does not delete in-app messages. Logging out or uninstalling does not delete the server account. Our messaging service processes business-notification content; this policy does not claim end-to-end encryption for notifications.

7. Permissions, clipboard, connections and external sharing

After you grant location access while using the app, weather can obtain a one-time current location as needed and send its coordinates to our weather endpoint. The app does not continuously track location in the background. Platform reverse-geocoding services process coordinates to display a city name. If location is unavailable or permission is denied, weather can fall back to an IP-derived city, or you can search for a city. Denying location does not affect unrelated features. You can revoke permission in system settings.

While recognizing share links in the foreground, or when you actively paste a code, the app may read clipboard text. Only links matching SnapFlow sharing formats or codes you actively submit are used to validate and open content; unmatched clipboard text is not uploaded as a share code. The system may prompt for clipboard access, which you can refuse.

FTP and similar account-connection or upload features process the relevant account, server and connection state to provide that feature. Protect connection credentials assigned to you and do not share them publicly. After you send a file or link through the system share sheet, recipients and selected platforms process copies under their own rules. Their further copying is not fully under our control.

8. Service providers and disclosure

We use these services as necessary for the features: Apple for sign-in and APNs; Tencent Cloud object storage and delivery services (COS/CDN/EdgeOne) to store or transmit uploaded media and related resources; and GitHub Pages to host this policy. Providers may receive necessary files, authentication or delivery information, IP addresses and request logs when processing requests.

Open-Meteo provides forecasts and city search. Our server rounds forecast coordinates to a regional grid of approximately 0.05 degrees before requesting a forecast; manually entered city searches are sent to its geocoding service. We do not send your account ID, phone number or Apple email to the weather provider. Weather coordinates are not written to your profile or feature analytics events. A manually selected city and its coordinates are saved on your device separately by account and environment to restore your selection.

We require providers processing personal information on our behalf to handle necessary data only for the agreed service purposes and provide protection no less than this policy and applicable rules require. Apple, GitHub and other independent services also have their own terms. We do not arbitrarily disclose personal information to unrelated third parties beyond your instructions, requested services, lawful safety handling or legal requirements.

See the Apple Privacy Policy, Tencent Cloud Privacy Policy and GitHub Privacy Statement. This policy website loads no advertising, analytics scripts or remote fonts and uses no cookies or local storage for language preferences. Its hosting provider may still process access logs.

9. Retention, security and processing locations

Account and cloud business records are retained for the periods needed to provide services and handle security, moderation, disputes or legal obligations. Local caches, preferences, progress and credentials may be stored in app storage or the system Keychain. Clearing caches does not delete cloud originals. Uninstalling generally removes the app container but does not guarantee removal of Keychain entries, server records or shared/exported copies.

Deletion in the interface may hide or soft-delete a record. Original files, review versions, logs and backups may not be physically erased immediately. We do not describe these actions as complete deletion of an account or all copies. For further access or deletion requests, we verify identity, handle data eligible for deletion and explain information that must be retained for law, safety or dispute handling.

We use encrypted transport, authentication and access controls to protect the service, but no storage or transmission is absolutely secure. Information may be processed in regions where we or providers operate services. For cross-border disclosure restricted by applicable law, we will provide required information, obtain authorization and apply necessary safeguards.

10. Your choices and deletion requests

You can use existing app controls to edit your profile, delete works or comments you manage, manage viewing history and recommendation preferences, unfollow, turn off synchronization, and revoke photo, camera or notification permissions in system settings. Revoking permission affects subsequent use of that feature; it does not automatically undo prior lawful processing or copies others have already saved.

For access, correction or deletion of your account and associated server data, contact dreamylost@outlook.com with the account identifier and request scope needed for verification. Do not send passwords, Apple credentials or full device tokens. The app includes a Settings → Account and Security → Delete Account page, but self-service deletion is not yet enabled. If the page says it is unavailable, use the email above to submit your request. We verify the requester’s identity and explain the outcome, any information that must be retained and why. Signing out, turning off notifications, deleting one message or uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Once self-service deletion is enabled, you must verify your current sign-in password or reauthorize a passwordless Apple account. An account with both a password and a linked Apple identity requires both checks. You must also confirm that deletion cannot be reversed. Acceptance immediately disables the account and prevents other devices from continuing to use old sessions. The processing target for associated data is usually within 7 days; the accepted request records the specific expected completion time. Acceptance does not mean all cleanup is complete, and passing the expected date does not automatically mean completion.

In a supporting version after enablement, Settings → Account Deletion Progress will let you query status, the expected time and the server-confirmed actual completion time without signing in again, using an opaque receipt saved in secure storage on this device. The receipt does not retain the former account’s name, phone number, email, password or Apple proof, and records are separated by service environment. Removing the local record does not cancel server-side deletion. Recovery of a local receipt is not guaranteed after uninstalling or changing devices; contact the developer if you need help.

After self-service deletion is enabled, albums you created will be tombstoned and all sharing stopped; you will only leave albums owned by others. Originals you uploaded, including contributions to others’ albums, and their shared references will be deleted. Only copies that others have already saved independently will be retained. Forwarding or referencing the same original is not an independent copy. Others’ uploaded originals and originals in the device system library are not deleted as part of your account deletion. We explain the scope of information that must be retained and why after verification. Independent copies others have already downloaded, captured or exported may be outside our control.

11. Children

SnapFlow is not directed to children under 13. Users below the local age for independently consenting to personal-information processing should use the service with guardian guidance and any necessary authorization. Contact us if you believe we processed a child’s information without appropriate authorization; we will investigate and handle deletion or restriction requests.

12. Updates and contact

This policy was updated and takes effect on November 21, 2026. We will update it as features and processing change. Material changes will be communicated through appropriate in-app or release channels. Processing that needs separate authorization does not automatically gain consent through a policy update. The Chinese and English versions describe the same processing scope.

For privacy, access, correction, deletion or children’s information questions, contact dreamylost@outlook.com. Include only information necessary to handle your request.
